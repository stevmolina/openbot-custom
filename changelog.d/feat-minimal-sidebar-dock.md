### Changed

- The bottom of the sidebar is smaller. The pill shows the server, Chats, a small stack of provider logos and More. Workflows, Tasks and Machines are no longer pinned by default; they are in More with the other pages. A page that you pinned stays pinned.
- The provider usage moved into the pill. The provider with the least left shows in front with its usage rings, and the others show as small logos behind it. Click the stack to see every provider, the amount left in each window, the reset times and a link to the Usage page.
- More is one short list of pages, then Help, Settings and Customize pill. Use Customize pill to pin a page to the pill.
