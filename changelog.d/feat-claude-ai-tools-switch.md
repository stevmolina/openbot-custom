### Added

- Settings > Providers > Claude has a new switch, "Use claude.ai tools". On, the Claude agents of the server can publish Artifacts to your claude.ai account and use your claude.ai connectors, such as Gmail, Notion and Slack. It needs a claude.ai sign-in and adds about 13k tokens to each request. It is off by default. A joined server shows the switch only when the server is also on the new version.
