### Changed

- The chat sidebar is now one list that scrolls. Folders sit between the chats with a quiet heading: the name and a small arrow, plus the chat count while the folder is closed. An open folder shows all of its chats, however many folders there are.
- A floating "unread" button at the bottom of the chat list counts the unread chats that are out of view, below the list or in a closed folder. A click opens that folder and scrolls to the next unread chat. This works on the desktop app, in the web app, and on joined servers.
