### Changed

- The sidebar is now a chat list. Each agent and channel is one row with its face, its name and its last message, newest first. A chat that waits for you shows what it waits for in place of the last message, and a dot marks a reply you did not read.
- The page rail is gone. Chats, Workflows and Tasks are in a pill at the bottom of the sidebar, and More opens Usage, Routines, Schedule, Marketplace, Help and Settings. The pin in More adds a page to the pill.
- The sidebar stays beside every page, also Workflows, Schedule, Usage, Marketplace and Settings.
- The account switch in the pill shows the logo of the open server, with your other server behind it. Its menu lists your servers, Settings and Add.
- The plan usage of each AI provider shows above the pill, with how much is left.
- Collapse the sidebar to a dock of faces. Hover a face to see its name and last message.
