### Added

- The agent dock comes out of the screen edge by itself when an agent asks you something, needs your approval or answers you. It slides out calmly, a soft halo marks the agent, and it does not take the focus. It stays out while a question waits, and for about 6 seconds after an answer. Turn it off with "Show when an agent needs you" in Settings > Agent dock.

### Changed

- A new agent dock now hides in the screen edge while you do not use it. Move the pointer to the edge to show it. A dock whose settings you already changed keeps your choice in "Hide when not in use".
