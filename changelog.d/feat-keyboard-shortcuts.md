### Added

- Keyboard shortcuts have one list. Press ⌘/ (Ctrl+/ on Windows and Linux), or ? outside a text field, to see every shortcut by group, with a filter. Help in the sidebar menu and the Help menu of the menu bar open it too.
- New shortcuts: ⌘N for a new chat, ⌥↑ and ⌥↓ for the previous and next chat, ⌥⇧↑ and ⌥⇧↓ for the previous and next unread chat, ⌘B to show or hide the sidebar, ⇧Esc to focus the message box, ⇧⌘C to copy the last reply, ⇧⌘; to copy its last code block, and ⌥⌘. to stop the agent of the open chat.
- Typing while nothing takes text, such as after a click on a chat, writes in the message box.
- The menu bar lists the shortcuts in the File, View and Help menus, and the Search, New and sidebar buttons show their keys in their tooltips.
