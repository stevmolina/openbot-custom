### Changed

- The "All machines" view of the chat list now looks like the chat list of one server: chats that need you are on top, and "Working", the folders of each machine and "Settled" are in the dock. A small mark at the end of each row tells you its machine.
- A row of machine chips over the "All machines" list shows all machines or one machine. The chosen chip shows the machine name, a dot marks a machine with a chat that needs you or an unread chat, and a machine that does not answer is dimmed. The choice stays after a restart.
